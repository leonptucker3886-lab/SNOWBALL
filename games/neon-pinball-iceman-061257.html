NEON PINBALL

Keep the ball alive with the flippers. Bumpers score 100 and slingshots 10. Light all 3 top lanes to raise your multiplier (up to x5). 3 balls.

Phone: hold the left or right half of the screen for each flipper. Hold the right half to charge the launcher, let go to fire.
Keyboard: Left/Z and Right/M for flippers, hold Space or Down to launch, P to pause.
