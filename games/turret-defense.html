TURRET DEFENSE

Creeps march down the neon path. Build turrets beside it to stop them. Kills earn credits; tap a turret you own to upgrade it (up to level 3). Every 5th wave has a boss. Lose all 20 lives and it's over.
Touch: pick a turret below, tap a dark tile to build, tap SEND WAVE. Keys: 1/2 pick turret, arrows/WASD move, Space build/upgrade, Enter send wave, P pause.
