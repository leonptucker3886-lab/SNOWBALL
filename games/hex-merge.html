HEX MERGE

Drop numbered neon hexes onto the honeycomb. Three or more matching hexes touching each other merge into one hex of the next number, and merges can chain. Keep merging before the board fills up!
Touch: tap an empty cell to drop. Keys: arrows / WASD to move the cursor, Space or Enter to drop, P to pause.
